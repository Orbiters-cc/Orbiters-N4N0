orbiters-capture: the capture worker of Orbiters - N4N0

  worker                        JSON-lines protocol on stdin/stdout (used by the app)
  record --source <src> [--out <sessions folder>] [--quality lossless|high|compact]
         [--frames N] [--seconds S] [--name <folder name>] [--mic <id, empty for the default>]
         [--game-audio-pid <pid>] [--tracking-hz <rate, records SteamVR poses>]
                                src = spout[:name] | raw:<raw capture folder> | test
  send-test [--name <sender>] [--width W] [--height H] [--fps F] [--depth-every N] [--seconds S]
                                publish a test pattern with valid strips as a Spout sender
  verify <raw capture folder> [--frames N]
                                decode a raw capture and compare it with its ground truth
  list-sources                  list Spout senders and microphones
