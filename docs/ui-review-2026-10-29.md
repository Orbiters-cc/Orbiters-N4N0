# UI review of the "Make N4N0 accessible" surfaces (29 October 2026, second pass)

A fresh look after the U1 (`docs/unity-projects-ui-2026-10-29.md`) and U2 (`docs/photos-sharing-ui-2026-10-29.md`)
passes: every surface the ticket added or changed, rendered at 1040, 1280 and 1920 points wide (settled states and
frames in the middle of their motion), side by side with Home, Videos and Settings and with each other.

Surfaces looked at: Unity projects (gallery, proposals, docked panel, narrow sheet, packages, repositories, new
project, the Add N4N0 setup flow, every loading, empty and error state), Capture (status cards with N4N0's card, the
avatar picker), Settings (VRChat account in all its states, Six more views while recording), Photos & captures (cards,
picture window, sharing panels, friends picker, explicit warning, requests, instance popover), Capture files,
Visited worlds (Share split, the world's page), the SteamVR overlay's share pages and N4N0's screen pages 30–33
(`--n4n0-menu`), toasts.

What already works well and was left alone: the static headers and their compact/below fits, the picture window's
shared-geometry morph and its panels, the friends picker's chips, the requests window, the setup flow's steps, the
empty states (each with its way in), skeletons and the activity capsule, the VRChat account card's two-factor cells.

## Notes for later passes

- egui's `ScrollArea::scroll_bar_rect` moves a vertical bar only along its axis: across, the bar stays at the
  area's own right edge. To put a bar in a gutter, widen the area into it and keep the content out with a margin
  (`projects::panel`).
- `Ui::with_visual_transform` scales and moves everything a scope draws (text, pictures, clip rects) but not where
  widgets take input: block presses while it is not the identity, and skip it once landed so text stays on the pixel
  grid.
